How to Make Pour-Over Coffee

Pour-over is a simple method that rewards patience. Here is how to make a great cup at home with a dripper, a filter and a kettle.

What you need

A dripper, paper filters, fresh medium-ground coffee, a scale and a gooseneck kettle.

Steps

Rinse the filter, add 20g of coffee, bloom with 40g of water for 30 seconds, then pour slowly in circles up to 320g total. Aim for a total brew time of about three minutes.
